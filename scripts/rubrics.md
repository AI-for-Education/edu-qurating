| #     | Dimension (score name)                      | Pairwise prompt                                                                                                                                          | What collapses into it                                                                                             |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1** | **Accuracy & Completeness**                 | “Which text presents the ideas more accurately and with fuller, well-supported coverage of the topic?”                                                   | All factual correctness, conceptual depth, absence of misconceptions.                                              |
| **2** | **Learning Facilitation**                   | “Which text makes it easier for a learner to understand *and* practise the ideas (clear structure, concrete examples, hints, checks-for-understanding)?” | Clarity, organisation, scaffolding, and near-term transfer—rolled into one measure of instructional effectiveness. |
| **3** | **Advancedness / Prior-Knowledge Required** | “Which text assumes *higher* prior knowledge or is better suited to older, more advanced learners?”                                                      | Sophistication of language, abstraction level, prerequisite concepts—lets you map to grade bands later.            |

Why these three
Completeness + correctness is the foundation; nothing else makes up for bad facts.

Learning facilitation soaks up the how of teaching (clarity, scaffolding, worked examples, immediate applicability). Empirically, this correlates only weakly with Raw Accuracy, so you still get an independent signal.

Advancedness gives you the primary-vs-secondary split you asked for; it is conceptually orthogonal to both factual quality and how well the material is taught.
