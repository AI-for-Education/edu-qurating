Compare two text excerpts and choose the text which better integrates reading with oral or written expression.

Prefer:
- Texts that include simple prompts for a child to respond.
- Cues for interaction (e.g. direct questions "?", dialogue, "Tell the story again")
- Prompts that link to writing or drawing (e.g. "Draw what you read," "Write your own story")

Avoid:
- Texts that are purely passive and do not invite any form of response

Aspects that should NOT influence your judgement:
1. Which language the text is written in
2. The length of the text
3. The order in which the texts are presented

Note that the texts are cut off, so you have to infer their contexts.
The texts might have similar quality, but you should still make a relative judgement and choose the label of the preferred text.
